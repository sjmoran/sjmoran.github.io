Research focus

Reliable AI, from representation to deployment.

Three questions connect most of my work.

  1. 01

    How should a system represent and retrieve information?

    Compact learned codes, approximate search, multi-modal retrieval, and the limits of vector search for production RAG.

  2. 02

    How do statistical systems become dependable tools?

    Evaluation, calibration, anomaly detection, code intelligence, and agent workflows designed for audit-heavy environments.

  3. 03

    Where can structure replace scale?

    Interpretable image operators, federated learning, machine unlearning, and small modules with useful inductive biases.
